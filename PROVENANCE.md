## Words

Every sentence of the pages' body text comes from https://www.riversidefamilyeyecare.com/, read from a crawl of 148 addresses on 5 October 2026 (UTC). The redesign's own words are interface labels, picture descriptions (alt text) and the notes this preview adds; the README lists them.

## Pictures and video made for this redesign

30 stills and 5 loops. None shows a person, the practice's premises, a clinical result or a named product. Every still and every loop poster was opened whole and then in enlarged regions at full resolution, looking for lettering, numbers, marks that read as a brand, a recognisable product, a person, a malformed object and a border. The stills are 2752 px wide; a page shows them at 900 px at most. Those that came back with lettering, a brand-like mark or a malformed object were made again. The files here are re-encodings made by this build (WebP through a browser canvas, H.264 without an audio track); they were read for metadata before publishing (README, Verification). Files up to 1,280 px wide are served for dense screens. 3 stills are used with a remark:

## Pictures and films from the live site

222 pictures published on the practice's site are used. Most stand on the page where the live site shows them; the redesign also shows a page's picture on the cards and menus that lead to that page. They are the practice's logo and photographs, the stock photographs of its articles, and the logos of the makers and insurers its pages name. 42 picture files (several are sizes of the same picture) could not be downloaded (37 refused with HTTP 403, 4 timed out with HTTP 504, 1 gone with HTTP 404) and are not here. The 3 films on `/eye-care-services/dry-eye-disease-and-treatment/`, `/team/jhonae-anglin/` are films the practice published on its own site, re-encoded smaller by this build.
