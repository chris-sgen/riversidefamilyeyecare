InMode Forma and Lumecca: A Breakthrough in Eye Care

Sep 12, 2025

If you live in Fort Myers, you know that the Florida sun, salty air, and long days on digital devices can take a toll on your eyes. Dryness, burning, and irritation are common complaints. These symptoms are more than inconvenient — they can impact quality of life, productivity, and even long-term eye health.

Request AppointmentCall 239-500-2020

That’s why Riverside Family Eye Care now offers InMode Forma-I and Lumecca IPL — two groundbreaking treatments that bring lasting relief while also supporting overall eye health. These non-invasive procedures go beyond quick fixes like artificial tears, addressing the root causes of dry eye and eyelid inflammation.

What Is InMode Forma-I?

Forma-I is a radiofrequency (RF) therapy that gently warms the delicate tissues around the eyes. This heat stimulates natural collagen production and helps unclog meibomian glands — the tiny oil-producing glands in your eyelids that play a crucial role in maintaining the health of your tears.

  • Comfortable, non-surgical treatment: Sessions feel like a warm massage around the eyes.
  • Improved tear quality: By restoring gland function, your eyes stay naturally lubricated.
  • Skin benefits: Patients often notice firmer, smoother skin around the eyes.

This dual benefit makes Forma-I a favorite among patients who want relief from the symptoms of dry eye while also addressing concerns like eyelid laxity or under-eye wrinkles.

What Is InMode Lumecca IPL?

Lumecca is an intense pulsed light (IPL) treatment designed to reduce inflammation and restore balance to the eyelids. For patients with chronic blepharitis or ocular rosacea — two common causes of dry eye — this treatment option can be life-changing.

  • Targets abnormal blood vessels that contribute to inflammation.
  • Kills harmful bacteria and mites that can worsen eyelid disease.
  • Quick, in-office sessions with little to no downtime.

Many patients notice clearer, brighter eyes and less irritation after just a few sessions.

How Do These Treatments Compare to Traditional Dry Eye Care?

Standard treatments (like artificial tears, warm compresses, and lid scrubs) help with short-term relief but often fail to provide lasting improvement. In contrast:

  • Forma-I restores gland health to improve tear quality naturally.
  • Lumecca IPL calms inflammation and addresses underlying skin and vascular issues.
  • Combination therapy ensures both oil gland dysfunction and surface inflammation are treated, giving patients the best chance at long-term relief.

What to Expect During Treatment

Knowing what happens at each step can help patients feel at ease. At Riverside Family Eye Care, treatments are quick, gentle, and designed with your comfort in mind.

1. Initial Consultation

Your visit starts with a comprehensive dry eye assessment. We’ll review your symptoms, check gland function, and decide whether Forma-I, Lumecca IPL, or both are best for your eyes.

2. The Procedure

  • Forma-I: A handheld device delivers warm radiofrequency energy to the area around the eyes. It feels soothing and takes about 30 minutes.
  • Lumecca IPL: Gentle pulses of light are applied to the skin around your eyes. Most patients describe it as a quick “snap” sensation.

3. Recovery

Both treatments are non-invasive with no downtime. You may notice mild redness, which fades within a few hours.

4. Treatment Plan

Results often improve with a series of 3–4 sessions, spaced a few weeks apart. Many patients experience noticeable relief after their first visit.

5. Long-Term Results

Because these treatments target root causes — not just surface dryness — results can last much longer than artificial tears or compresses.

Why Patients in Fort Myers Choose Riverside Family Eye Care for Dry Eye Care

Our compassionate team is dedicated to personalized care. We’ve invested in advanced technology like InMode Forma-I and Lumecca IPL because we believe our patients deserve solutions that go beyond temporary relief.

We’ve helped patients ranging from busy parents to retirees regain comfort and confidence in their vision. Our focus is always on listening, understanding, and tailoring care for everyone.
