What Is Cataract Co-Management?

Cataract co-management is a collaborative approach between your trusted optometrist and a skilled cataract surgeon. Together, they ensure you receive seamless care before, during, and after surgery.

At Riverside Family Eye Care, our optometrists handle your pre-surgical exams, guide you in choosing the right lens for your vision needs, and oversee your recovery for healthy healing. This approach lets you continue seeing the doctors you trust while benefiting from the expertise of leading cataract surgeons in Fort Myers.

By coordinating every step of the process, you can expect:

  • A personalized treatment plan tailored to your lifestyle and visual needs
  • Smooth communication between your optometrist and surgeon
  • Convenient follow-up care right here in Fort Myers
  • Better long-term visual outcomes and peace of mind

Our Approach to Cataract Co-Management at Riverside Family Eye Care

At Riverside Family Eye Care, cataract co-management means you’re never alone in your journey toward clear vision. From diagnosis to post-surgical care, our optometrists stay by your side, ensuring every step is personalized and supported. Here’s how our process works:

  • Comprehensive Eye Exams: We begin with a detailed eye exam to confirm your cataract diagnosis and discuss whether surgery is the right choice for your vision and lifestyle.
  • Collaborative Planning: Once you’re ready, we coordinate your referral to trusted cataract surgeons in Fort Myers who use advanced surgical techniques and premium lens implant options.
  • Post-Surgical Follow-Up: After your cataract surgery in Fort Myers, you’ll return to our office for follow-up visits. During this post-operative care, we monitor your healing, manage your prescriptions, and ensure your eyes remain comfortable and healthy.

This continuity of care helps prevent complications and promotes long-lasting visual clarity. Our goal is simple: to make cataract care feel comfortable, informed, and completely centered on your best vision.

Cataract Surgery Co-Management FAQs

How do I know if I need cataract surgery?

If you’re experiencing cloudy or blurred vision, glare around lights, or trouble driving at night, you may have cataracts. Your optometrist at Riverside Family Eye Care will examine your eyes to confirm the diagnosis and determine if surgery is the best option for your clouded lens.

Who performs cataract surgery in Fort Myers?

We partner with trusted cataract surgeons in Fort Myers who use the latest laser and lens technology for safe, precise results. You’ll have surgery performed by a board-certified ophthalmologist, while your ongoing eye care remains with our experienced optometrists.

How long does it take to recover from cataract surgery?

Most patients notice improved vision within a few days. Your eyes typically heal within several weeks, and follow-up visits at Riverside Family Eye Care ensure everything is progressing well and your vision remains clear.
